# Design decisions (Grade 3, from the playbook agreed with Jovan, 25 November 2026)

| Topic | Decision |
| --- | --- |
| Exam | Grade 3 ESL End of Semester 1, Cambridge Primary ESL Stage 4, SY 2026-27. |
| Units | Our Community; Earth and Beyond; Homes; Food (the school's pacing guide, Semester 1). |
| Scope | Full, like Grade 5: 9 stops + the Summit. Speaking is NOT included. |
| Stops | 1 Word Vault, 2 Time Machine, 3 Grammar Toolkit, 4 Listening Lounge (pictures and a form), 5 Chat Café (short conversations), 6 Notice Board (signs and replies), 7 Gap Garage, 8 Reading Lighthouse, 9 Writing Workshop. Every exam part is trained in a stop. |
| Listening | 15 questions as on the real paper: Part 1 pictures (5), Part 2 five short conversations (5), Part 3 a form (5, one spelled name). Every recording plays two times on a test paper, like the exam. |
| Reading and Use of English | 34 questions: Part 1 signs (5), Part 2 best reply (6), Part 3 circle the word (6), Part 4 a recount with three options per gap (7), Part 5 who says it (5), Part 6 an article (5). |
| Writing | Task 1 describe a person, Task 2 describe a place or thing; 6 to 8 sentences (40 to 70 words) each. Marked with the school's real Grade 3 rubric: five categories, 0 to 2 marks each, 10 per task (Task achievement and content, Organisation and layout, Grammar range and accuracy, Vocabulary and spelling, Writing conventions). |
| Sign-in | Student / Teacher / Visitor; classes 3B01 to 3B09; progress codes; How to use guide; school label "Vinschool Times City T36 Primary School – Advanced Programme". |
| Look | The Grade 5 paper look with ink cartoon animation, black line-drawing pictures (Fluent Emoji high-contrast), Map and Stops buttons, listening exam mode. |
| Summit | Open for everyone. Full practice exam: Listening 15 + Reading and Use of English 34 = 49 marks; trophy at 35 (70%); "Practise again" names the stop for each Part. Writing paper on its own card (2 tasks, 35 minutes, self-marked). |
| Teacher-only labels | The objective code and DOK label under questions show for teachers and TAs only. |
| Saved data | Keys start with `g3-esl-trail-` so the Grade 3, 4 and 5 trails never share stars on one device. |
| Item security | Analyst and checker only open the real papers. The list of reserved situations from the papers is kept privately, outside this package. |
| Publishing | A private claude.ai link, this GitHub repository (website), and zip backups in the grade's Drive folder. |

## Lessons from Grade 5 built in from the start
Summit totals from the ESL papers; a Summit that does not repeat any stop's texts or writing prompts; no answer-length pattern (the right answer is the longest option in about a third of questions); real grammatical distractors only; listening distractors all heard in the talk; hints that teach a strategy; every correct typed answer accepted; honest DOK labels; definition-based Words step; writing models with marks per category; clearer recordings (exact letter names, equal loudness); separate saved-data keys; British spelling; no em dashes.
